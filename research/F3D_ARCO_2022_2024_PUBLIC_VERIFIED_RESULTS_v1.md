# F3-D: Independent replay of historical ARCO/ENTSO-E matched-service calculations

## Source status and scope

Observed-data weather and market source years: **2022 and leap-year 2024**. Official Copernicus ERA5 ARCO is the reanalysis point representation, **not** original CDS monthly NetCDF bytes or measured site weather. 2023 full-year Copernicus ARCO matched original source CDS-derived farm power at 1-W resolution over 8,760 UTC hours.

The export was generated by re-running the source-calendar/hash checks and independent hourly PtXLens physical/cost replay locally; the original hourly data, private primal witnesses, provider XML/NetCDF and authentication tokens were never copied.

## Independent validation

Each of the four original hourly E/E+S primal solutions and the price-neutral E service-selector witness were re-audited by PtXLens. The audit checked H2 delivery, mass and electricity balances, source-year grid settlement, compressor duty and the Type I 20–200 bar CoolProp hydrogen EOS screening extrema. Both year-specific price and wind provider source calendars/hashes were rechecked.

**Limitation:** HiGHS-reported cost lower bounds and the selector's global optimality gap were checked arithmetically but their optimizer dual certificates were **not independently solved or reconstructed**. The interval is a **solver-reported** economic savings bound, not an independently peer-certified confidence interval.

## Economic comparability and limitations

Within one year and one capacity interpretation, E and E+S have identical hourly modelled H2 service. **Across** years or storage capacity interpretations, the maximum price-blind feasible service fraction can differ. Compare EUR/kg of modelled delivered H2 as well as annual nominal EUR; do not attribute differences solely to prices.

2022 and 2024 prices are nominal source-calendar EUR, not CPI-normalized. The figures are operational net-grid electricity-cost differences **before** incremental storage installation CAPEX, fixed/variable maintenance OPEX, financing, taxes, plant construction, risk or decommissioning costs. There are no manufacturer quotes or verified native BAFU whole-plant inventories in this evidence.

No measured industrial H2 operation, whole-plant investment NPV, full LCA/LCIA GWP or scientific v1.0 release is certified. This is a reproducible numerical source-model study.

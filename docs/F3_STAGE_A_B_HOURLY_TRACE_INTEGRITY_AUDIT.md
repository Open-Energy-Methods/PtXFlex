# F3 Stage A → Stage B hourly evidence integrity (2026-11-14)

**Programme:** OEM-PTX-2026-01. **Status:** implemented integrity control; original-source re-execution and downstream artifact re-pinning pending. This is not a new measured-plant result, nor complete F2/F3/L1/L2/L3.

## Discovered scientific failure mode

Previously, Stage B checked the Stage A original-source 2023 SHA identifiers, annual additional shortage (kg), number of shortage hours and certification interval. Those checks did **not** bind the 8,760 hourly shortages to the Stage A certificate. A temporal permutation preserving annual shortage and count of shortage hours could change peak 24h demand, contiguous event durations and backstop ramp requirements while passing the old checks. Those metrics are precisely the information needed for future source-backed supply sizing. The original PyNEXUS E and E+S SHA checks do not cryptographically authenticate a newly solved Stage A hourly trace.

## Implemented correction

1. Stage A serializes its full 8,760h additional-shortage trace in a deterministic UTF-8 JSON form (sorted keys, compact separators, trailing newline, nonfinite numbers rejected), computes SHA-256 of those **exact bytes**, and stores the digest alongside the Stage A scenario summary. Its trace artifact is written with those exact bytes.
2. Stage A certificate validation requires and carries the 64-hex digest for each certified executable scenario; uncertified results are not assigned a fictitious trace digest.
3. Stage B recomputes the digest from the supplied trace and rejects a mismatch **before** any 24h peak, contiguous event, or top-up sizing is calculated. It also rejects NaN/non-JSON values with a typed evidence error.
4. Mathematical-only negative tests reorder two hourly shortage values without changing annual kg or the number of shortage hours; the trace must now be rejected. Missing, malformed and substituted digests also fail closed.

This protects the **derived hourly sequence**, not the physical adequacy of a hypothetical hydrogen supplier. The E 20t source-based pressure screen remains inadequate for the exact original E-delivered 2023 hourly reference under the currently screened 200bar bank assumptions; E+S feasibility does not establish whole-plant economic or environmental superiority.
